Bullying is a problem.

Yet somehow the rates of bullying only go up.

💡

Our solution?

Instead of only focusing on how to stop the bully, which typically only makes them act more sneaky, let's add on another layer of prevention: let's help the victim to take back their power of the situation, and learn what they CAN do.

Let's teach our children the lifelong skills of how to deal with difficult people. Because these situations don't stop in childhood. They continue in life with the coworker, boss, and family members.

Learning how to deal with difficult people is a skill that lasts a lifetime.

Meet the author

Bella Weiss, LPC, CMHC

  • Child Anxiety, Attachment, and Trauma Therapy
  • Parenting Therapist
  • Lakewood, NJ
  • Author, The Bullies (Feldheim, 2026)

Bella Weiss, LPC CMHC is a psychotherapist who works in private practice specializing in children and teens struggling with anxiety and trauma. On the side, she spends time promoting The Bullies book, which is based on Izzy Kalman's Bullies to Buddies program. She has seen just how helpless parents and educators feel when their children go through challenging social situations. After selling out over 2000 copies in its first ten weeks after it was published, The Bullies book solutions have helped countless children take back their power in social settings and see other kids enjoying regular social interactions with them. It gives parents and educators guidance on how to help children who are going through the challenge of having other children acting meanly to them.
